# Example prompts

## Topic mining
- Find me an easy NLP thesis topic. I have a free Colab T4 and 6 months.
- Give me 10 thesis ideas in low-resource language NLP and eliminate the weak ones with evidence.

## Idea autopsy
- Is "Bangla sentiment analysis with LLMs" still publishable? Try to kill it.
- I want to combine X and Y. Has this been done? Show closest work and overlap.

## Base paper to extension
- Here is a paper. Fill a paper card, list weaknesses, and rank three extensions.

## Design and feasibility
- Check Colab feasibility for fine-tuning <model> on <dataset> and design baselines and ablations.
- Scaffold the notebooks for my project.

## Statistics
- Compare my proposed model with the baseline using paired bootstrap and report CIs.

## Writing
- Write the Results section from my ledger only. Mark missing numbers as [RESULT REQUIRED].
- Lint my draft against the ledger.

## Review and submission
- Review my manuscript as three skeptical reviewers.
- Suggest journals, starting from where the closest papers were published, and verify scope and APC.
