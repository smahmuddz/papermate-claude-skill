# Journal targeting

Goal: find journals where this specific paper has a realistic chance, not the most prestigious names. Everything about a journal that can change (scope, metrics, APC, turnaround, policies) must be checked live; training memory is stale.

## Procedure
1. **Characterize the paper**: contribution type (method, resource, empirical study, negative result), task/domain, depth of experiments, strength of novelty (honest rating from the scorecard).
2. **Harvest candidates from evidence**: where were the closest papers from recon published? Which journals publish similar methodology and depth? Which journals cite the base paper's venue?
3. **Verify each candidate on the journal's own pages** (homepage, aims and scope, guide for authors, recent issues, editorial board). Fetch them.
4. **Check recent similar papers**: open 3-5 recent articles in the journal that resemble yours in task, method, and experimental depth. Does the bar look comparable?
5. **Check practicalities**: open access vs subscription, APC (and waivers), indexed in what, review/first-decision times if published, submission requirements.
6. **Screen for risk**: predatory or mismatched venues, special-issue traps, journals that recently changed scope, journals delisted from major indexes. Be conservative; say what you verified.
7. **Rank by fit and expected difficulty**, with a safe/target/reach spread.

## Rules
- Do not state an impact factor, CiteScore, APC, or acceptance rate from memory. Report only what you fetched, with the date.
- Do not promise acceptance or imply likelihood with false precision; use difficulty bands.
- Elsevier first when the user asks, but if no Elsevier journal fits well, say so and offer alternatives from other publishers or conference/workshop routes.
- Remind the user to follow the chosen journal's guide for authors for formatting, declarations, and AI-use disclosure, and to submit to one journal at a time.
- If the paper's novelty rating is 5 or below, say that top-tier targets are unrealistic and recommend venues where solid, reproducible work in the area is valued.
